Privacy Policy for Wrist Paddle

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Wrist Paddle ("the App," "we," "us") is developed by Armrest Games. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Summary

Wrist Paddle is designed to collect as little data as possible. There are no third-party advertising networks, no user tracking, and no account creation required to play. Wrist Paddle syncs only your best score through your own iCloud account (Apple iCloud key-value storage). The developer cannot see it. No other data is collected.

Information We Collect

Motion Data

The App uses your Apple Watch's built-in accelerometer and gyroscope to detect wrist movement, which controls gameplay. This motion data is processed entirely on your device in real time and is never transmitted, stored, or shared anywhere. It exists only for the moment it takes to move your in-game paddle.

Gameplay Data

The App stores the following on your device with UserDefaults. Settings and scores are also synced between your Apple Watch and your iPhone with WatchConnectivity:

  • Your best score, rank, and progress
  • Unlocked skins, sounds, and customization preferences
  • App settings (sensitivity, sound preferences, control scheme)

This data is not accessible to us. We do not operate servers that receive or store your gameplay data. Your best score is kept on the device and, when iCloud is available, synced as described under iCloud. We do not collect it.

Purchase Information

If you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. On your device, StoreKit tells the App that the purchase completed so it can unlock the corresponding content. We do not collect that purchase.

Game Center

If you sign into Game Center (optional), your score for a normal game is submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboard under your Game Center player identity. This is handled entirely by Apple's GameKit framework under Apple's own privacy terms. We do not collect, store, or process your Game Center identity or that score on any server of ours (we operate no servers). The App does not download the leaderboard into its own storage. When you open the leaderboard, Apple's Game Center screen shows it. Ranks inside the App are calculated on your device from your best score.

iCloud

Wrist Paddle syncs only your best score through your own iCloud account, using Apple iCloud key-value storage. The developer cannot see it: the score stays in your iCloud account, it is not sent to Armrest Games, and we have no way to read it. No other data is collected. If iCloud is not available, the best score stays on the device. If the device and iCloud both have a best score, the App keeps the higher one.

That best score is also sent to the Wrist Paddle app on your paired iPhone. That copy stays on your devices. We do not collect it.

Analytics

The App does not collect analytics. We may look at Apple's built-in App Analytics in App Store Connect for aggregate, anonymized patterns such as retention and drop-off. Apple provides that data in aggregate form and it does not identify you individually. We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs.

Notifications

If you opt in, the App schedules a local daily reminder on your device for the time you choose. The reminder stays on the device. The App does not use push notifications and does not collect a device token. You can turn the reminder off at any time in the App's Settings or your device's system settings.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not display third-party advertisements.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or tracking technologies.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email, or other personal identifiers unless you voluntarily contact us for support.

Children's Privacy

Wrist Paddle is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not include user-generated content, chat features, or any way for users to communicate with one another.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your best score is stored on the device and, when iCloud is available, in your own iCloud key-value storage. Other gameplay data, including settings, is stored on the device. Deleting the App removes the on-device copy. The copy in your iCloud account remains there until it is removed from iCloud.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in an updated "Last updated" date above.
