How to play

The game is on Apple Watch. The iPhone app is the companion: best score, skins, ranks, the shop, and settings. The Watch app can run on its own.

Rest your forearm on a chair arm, desk, or table. Open Wrist Paddle on your Watch and tap Start. Twist your wrist slightly to move the paddle. The ball bounces off the wall on the left. Tap the screen to pause, then resume or quit to the menu.

A normal game starts with 3 lives. Each hit scores 250 points. Easy Mode, in the iPhone Settings tab, uses 5 lives and does not change scoring or speed. The first launch shows a short lesson. How to Play on the Watch home screen opens it again.

The game calibrates to the posture your arm is already in when you tap Start, Zen Mode, or Play Again. Only your wrist needs to move. If the watch tips away from that posture, play pauses and the screen says HOLD FLAT until you return.

Zen Mode

Zen Mode is practice. It does not keep score, it does not take lives, and the speed stays where you set it. Zen results are not saved as a best score, do not change your rank, and are not submitted to Game Center.

Controls

The paddle moves the wrong way.
The Watch detects which wrist you use. To override it, open Wrist Paddle on iPhone, go to Settings, turn on "Set wrist manually," and pick a wrist. You can also turn on "Invert paddle direction" there, or on the Watch under Sensitivity.
The game keeps saying HOLD FLAT.
Your watch has tipped away from the resting posture captured when you tapped Start. Rest your forearm somewhere comfortable, quit to the menu, and tap Start again from that position. Flatness Tolerance in the iPhone Settings tab changes how far your arm can drift before this pause.
The paddle feels too twitchy or too sluggish.
Change sensitivity on the Watch (Sensitivity on the home screen) or in the iPhone Settings tab. The change applies right away.
Play without wrist motion.
On iPhone, open Settings and turn on "Use Digital Crown instead of wrist motion." The paddle then follows the Digital Crown.
There is no sound.
In the iPhone app, Settings, "Mute in-game sound" should be off. The Watch silent switch and Theater Mode also silence the game, because game audio is mixed with whatever else you are playing. Sound can route to Bluetooth headphones when those are connected.

Scores, ranks, and skins

Your best score is saved on the Watch and sent to the iPhone app when a normal game ends. The iPhone Home tab stays empty until that first score arrives. Ranks follow your score. Free ranks are Rookie, Deflector, Rally Master, and Sharp Eye. Eight skins are earned by score without paying.

Motion is read on the Watch to move the paddle during the lesson and during a round. It is not part of an account. There is no Wrist Paddle account.

Game Center

Signing in to Game Center is optional. If you are already signed in on the device, a normal game's score can be submitted to the Wrist Paddle leaderboard when the game ends. You can play the whole game signed out. The iPhone Progress tab shows your ranks and, when Game Center is signed in, opens the global leaderboard. Zen Mode scores are not submitted.

Purchases

Wrist Paddle is free. The iPhone Shop offers two optional one-time purchases. They are non-consumable: you buy each one once, and there is no subscription and no consumable currency. The Watch app does not sell anything. Prices are shown by the App Store.

  • Tier 2 — Unlock Everything. Multi-ball play (a second ball at 3,000 points and a third at 7,000), the difficulty climb past the free cap, 8 more ranks (12 total), 14 more skins (22 total), and custom colors.
  • Tier 3 — Founder's Pass. Everything in Tier 2, plus 9 exclusive skins, the Founder rank, an exclusive set of hit sounds, a Founder badge on share cards, and, as the Shop screen states, all future content.

If a purchase does not show up on the Watch, leave both apps open for a few seconds so the unlock can sync. Then, on iPhone, open Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Restore Purchases is also in Settings. Use the same Apple ID that paid.

Refunds are requested from Apple. If Apple refunds a purchase, the unlocked content is removed.

Notifications

An optional daily reminder can ask if you can beat your best score. It is off unless you turn it on in the iPhone Settings tab, and it is scheduled on the iPhone at the time you pick. It is not a remote push. Turn it off in the app or in system settings.
